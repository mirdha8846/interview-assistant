### Troubleshooting Content
```
# Troubleshooting

## "License Invalid" Error
Your Device ID may have changed. This can happen if you:
- Reinstalled Windows
- Changed major hardware components
- Contact support for a free license transfer

## "No Audio Detected"
1. Make sure audio is playing through your speakers/headphones
2. The app captures system audio, not microphone
3. Try playing a YouTube video to test
4. Restart the app

## "AI Not Responding"
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify your Google API key is valid
3. Check API quota (free tier: 15 requests/minute)
4. Try again in a minute

## App Shows Warning on Launch
Windows SmartScreen may block the app because it's new.
1. Click "More info"
2. Click "Run anyway"
This is safe - the app is not malicious.

## Overlay Not Showing
1. Press Shift+T to toggle visibility
2. The overlay may be off-screen - restart the app
3. Make sure you're not in a fullscreen game/app
```
